# Hours and location

## Opening hours
Monday to Friday: 9:00 am – 5:00 pm.
Saturday: 9:00 am – 2:00 pm.
Sunday: closed.
Holiday hours are posted on our website one week ahead.

## Address and directions
BrightSmile Dental Studio, 123 Douglas Street, Victoria, BC.
We are on the second floor; the elevator entrance is on the
Johnson Street side of the building.

## Parking
Free patient parking behind the building (6 stalls, marked
"BrightSmile"). Street parking on Johnson Street is metered.
