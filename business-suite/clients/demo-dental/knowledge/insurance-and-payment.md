# Insurance and payment

## Direct billing
Yes, we direct-bill most major Canadian dental insurance plans,
including Pacific Blue Cross, Sun Life, Manulife and Canada Life.
Bring your insurance card to your first visit and we will check
your coverage before treatment starts.

## What insurance does not cover
Cosmetic services such as teeth whitening are usually not covered
by insurance. We always tell you the full cost before any treatment,
so there are no surprises.

## Payment options
We accept Visa, Mastercard, Interac e-Transfer and cash.
For treatment plans over $1,000 we offer a 0% payment plan over
6 months, arranged before treatment begins.
