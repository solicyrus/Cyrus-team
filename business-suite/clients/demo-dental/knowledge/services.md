# Our services

## Dental cleaning and checkup
A full cleaning, polish, and a checkup exam with X-rays if needed.
Most visits take about 60 minutes. We recommend a cleaning and
checkup every 6 months for most patients.

## Teeth whitening
Professional in-office teeth whitening in a single visit of about
90 minutes. We also sell take-home whitening kits at reception and
by mail order.

## Invisalign consultation
A free 30-minute Invisalign consultation: we scan your teeth, show
you a preview of the expected movement, and give you a written
quote. No obligation to start treatment.

## Dental implants
Implant consultations include a 3D scan and a written treatment
plan with the full cost before you decide anything.

## Kids dentistry
Gentle first visits for children aged 3 and up. Kids' appointments
are shorter (30 minutes) and parents can stay in the room.
