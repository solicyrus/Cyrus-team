# Frequently asked questions

## What happens at a first visit?
Your first visit is a cleaning and checkup: we take X-rays if
needed, clean and polish your teeth, and the dentist does a full
exam. Plan for about 60 minutes, and bring your insurance card if
you have one.

## I am nervous about dental visits. Can you help?
Yes. Tell us when you book and we will schedule a longer, quieter
appointment, explain every step before it happens, and agree on a
stop signal. You are always in control.

## What if I have a dental emergency?
Call us at +1-250-555-0142. We keep same-day emergency slots every
morning for patients with pain, a broken tooth, or swelling.
Outside opening hours, the message on that number names the
on-call dentist.

## Do you see children?
Yes — kids dentistry for ages 3 and up, with parents welcome in
the room.
