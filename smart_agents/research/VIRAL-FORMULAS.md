## THE 30-SECOND GOLDEN FORMULA (standing, from VIRAL-PROTOCOL.md)

0–3s HOOK → 3–15s TENSION → 15–25s CLIMAX → 25–30s LOOP & CTA.
The final line must flow back into the hook line (narrative
loop). Footage looping is forbidden — this is a words-only
device.

## HOOK FORMULAS (proven patterns)

1. **Clarity + surprise in one line** (Kallaway pattern):
   line one states the topic instantly AND gives a reason to
   care (pain, benefit, surprise). Ex: "The tech in the Vegas
   Sphere is insane. Biggest screen ever built — 20x bigger
   than an IMAX." Then re-fire attention with a pattern
   interrupt word: "But…", "However…".
2. **Superlative / biggest-first**: "The biggest X in <place>,
   just got bigger." Scale is the interrupt.
3. **Specific-number claim**: a real, concrete number in the
   first line (a saving, a count, a size). Numbers must be
   TRUE and sourced — our fact gate kills invented ones.
4. **Secret reveal + how-to promise**: "SECRET <thing> IN
   <place> AND HOW TO FIND IT" as on-screen text over the
   strongest visual; numbered steps carry retention.
5. **Moment capture**: promise a one-time event ("the moment
   the Sphere came alive") — scarcity of the moment is the hook.
6. **Object-in-hand shock**: hold the thing, state the fact,
   ask the outrage question. One take can beat editing.
7. **Mistake countdown**: "N dumb mistakes to avoid in
   <place>" — the countdown is the pacing engine; viewers
   wait for #1 and validate in comments.

## PACING RULES

- Strongest visual FIRST. No wind-up, no greeting, no logo.
- One new visual fact per spoken line; montage/proof sections
  cut every 1–3 seconds.
- Numbered steps or a countdown re-hook the viewer at every
  step — use them in the Tension beat.
- Proof by showing beats assertion: name the visible evidence.
- On-screen text mirrors the hook (many viewers start muted).

## PAYOFF RULES

- The climax (15–25s) cashes EXACTLY what the hook promised —
  no bait-and-switch (comments punish it, QA rejects it).
- Specifics close the deal: itemise, compare, show the total.

## LOOP & CTA RULES

- One decisive CTA only: a decision ("book this, not that")
  or a question viewers can answer in one line ("Could you
  finish it?") — question CTAs farm comments, which farm reach.
- After the CTA, the closing line re-opens the opening
  question so an auto-replay feels continuous (the
  confusion→rewatch lesson of the 5M-view Sphere clip: end by
  re-asking, not by concluding).

## WHAT THE ALGORITHMS PAY FOR (craft consensus, 2026-10-27)

- Completion rate and rewatch rate first; ~85%+ completion is
  associated with breakout Shorts.
- Engagement velocity in the first hours — shares to
  trip-planning friends are the travel niche's strongest
  signal (the 2.0M-play deal video: 39.2K shares).

## ANTI-PATTERNS (QA rejects on sight)

- Vague openings ("amazing places you must see") with no
  anchor: no number, no superlative, no secret, no question.
- Endings that trail off or stack multiple CTAs.
- Invented prices/hours/phones — hard fail, always.
- Any footage loop or padding to reach 30s.
